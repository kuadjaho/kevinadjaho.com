Flagship project

e-Port.bj: connecting the Cotonou port community

The stakes

The Port of Cotonou needed to move from a set of siloed systems to a genuine digital ecosystem: a common backbone connecting customs, the single window, stevedores and terminals, secure and aligned with the sector's international requirements, including the IMO FAL Convention. Project Lead of the Port Information System Portal (e-Port.bj) at the Autonomous Port of Cotonou (PAC) since Nov. 2024, I lead the implementation of the Port Information System Portal (PSIP): single sign-on (SSO), digital services, electronic document management and secure exchanges.

Governance

With the PAC's directorates, I drafted the terms of reference and the functional specifications for the portal's modules, as well as the memorandum of understanding signed in September 2025 by the port community stakeholders. Day to day, I coordinate the technical and business teams of customs, terminals, banks, freight forwarders and state agencies, and I work with the experts of Port of Antwerp-Bruges International (POABI), who have supported the programme since 2022, on the governance and operating strategy of the portal.

Structuring choices

Single sign-on and secure exchanges built on open standards (OAuth2), with national PKI integration planned; integrations with Customs (WEBB) and GUCE.bj, then with terminal operators and banks; import processes redesigned upstream, from 2022, and worked on in workshops with port stakeholders, so that the portal serves procedures that are already shared.

Risks addressed

Regulatory compliance, followed with ASIN (security audit before go-live) and with APDP. The security of inter-agency exchanges, through authentication and encryption. Adoption, with a change management plan to anchor the platform in the institution. Delivery commitments, through contract follow-up and acceptance of deliverables.

Results

Import processes redesigned in 2022 within the SIP team, twelve of which I wrote and modelled, then submitted to the port community in the pre-validation workshops whose format I proposed (September and October 2022). The portal and the document library passed technical acceptance in late 2025 and were validated in January 2026, then single sign-on was accepted and validated in September 2026. Go-live follows the ASIN security audit. Internationally, I took the initiative for Benin's request for IMO assistance on its Maritime Single Window, then followed it as the PAC focal point through to the IMO technical assistance mission at the Port of Cotonou (Dec. 2023), under the FAL Convention.
